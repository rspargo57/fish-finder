🎣 Welcome to Fish Finder

Where should you fish tomorrow? Every night, 16 signals — captain reports, whales, bait, water breaks, weather — fuse into one score per spot. The picks get sharper the longer it runs.

🎣
1. Pick your mode
Header: 🎣 Inshore or 🐟 Offshore. Offshore covers tuna, swordfish, mahi, wahoo, marlin, sailfish, sharks — everything canyon & pelagic. Click active mode again for the "both" view.
📖
2. Read Today's Report
Top tab. Your full nightly brief — tomorrow's picks, best fishing windows, 7-day outlook, top reachable zones, departure times. Rebuilds every night at 8pm ET.
🗺️
3. Read the map
Dots: blue = cold · green = steady · orange/red = on fire. Circles = tuna, diamonds = stripers. Cyan/orange lines = 68/72°F breaks. Blue/green lines = chlorophyll edges. Where they cross, a ⭐ gold star marks a Golden Zone — the strongest tuna signal on the chart.
👆
4. Click any spot
A popup opens with weather at that spot (6 AM & 2 PM wind + waves + sky + temp + rain chance), distance from your port, fleet chatter, and the time to leave the dock for a daybreak arrival.
⚓
5. Make it yours
Click the ⚓ chip top-right. Pick your port (8 presets), set your boat's tank/MPG/cruise speed/caps. Every distance, fuel number, and departure time recalculates around your setup.

In Today's Report, see which zones this region's captain channels are talking about this week. 2+ channels mentioning a zone = corroborated, and the model gives that zone a real boost in the pick score.

🎣
My Catches — log every fish
Near the bottom of Today's Report (below the picks + planner). Log any catch — date, zone, species, size, kept vs released, method, water temp, notes. Whether or not you pre-planned the trip. "Your Season So Far" summarizes as you go. Hit 📋 Copy log for Captain and paste to me in chat — I'll turn your real catches into evidence that tunes the model.
📈
Trip Planner accuracy
When a planned trip's date arrives, the plan card shows "How'd it go?" — went & caught / went & skunked / stayed home / didn't happen. Records to your trip archive. A running right N of last M scorecard tracks how often the Trip Planner's verdict matched what really happened.
📚
It gets sharper over time
Every night's picks + 16 signals get archived, along with the 7-day look-ahead so we can score predictions against what actually happens. Add your logged catches on top of that, and the model gets sharper every week. First accuracy analysis: August 12.

Tides & Striper Windows · mostly for stripers · New London

Loading tides…
Stripers live and die by the tide — moving water at structure = feeding. Peak flow = aggressive bite. Slack tide (20–30 min around H/L) = big-fish window. The Race and Plum Gut fire on the ebb. Offshore tuna are much less tide-driven — SST breaks and bait matter more out there.

The blue-green edge is where tuna hunt — plankton drives bait, bait drives tuna. When you toggle chlorophyll on, the blue line is the 0.15 mg/m³ edge (outer, clear water) and the green line is the 0.30 mg/m³ edge (inner, rich water). Fish the water between them, especially where it crosses the SST break.
